Moving Average Crossover • Educational Simulator

How this strategy works

This is a classic Moving Average Crossover strategy:

  • Buy signal → when the short-term SMA crosses above the long-term SMA (bullish crossover)
  • Sell signal → when the short-term SMA crosses below the long-term SMA (bearish crossover)

The simulator generates synthetic price data that roughly follows a random walk with mild trend, calculates the two SMAs, detects crossovers, and tracks a simple long-only portfolio (buy on signal, sell on opposite signal, stay in cash otherwise).

Note: This is for educational purposes only. Real markets have slippage, commissions, overnight gaps, and many other factors not modeled here.
